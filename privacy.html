Lucky Numbers — Privacy Policy

Effective September 1, 2026

What we collect

Lucky Numbers only collects what you give it to make the app work:

  • Account info — your email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Profile and goals — age, height, weight, activity level, goal, and target dates you enter during onboarding.
  • Health and fitness logs — meals and their macros, body weight, sleep hours, daily checklist items, supplement/compound entries, and progress photos you choose to upload.
  • AI conversations — messages you send to the AI coach and food photos or descriptions you submit for macro estimation.
  • Notifications — if you enable them, a push subscription token for your device and your timezone.

We do not collect location data, contacts, advertising identifiers, or analytics beyond basic server logs.

How your data is used

Your data is used for exactly one thing: running the app for you — building your plan, tracking your progress, grading your days, and answering your coach questions. We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for marketing.

Where it lives

Data is stored with Supabase (our database and authentication provider) on infrastructure hosted in the United States. Access is protected by row-level security: your records are readable and writable only by your signed-in account. Progress photos are stored in a storage bucket tied to your account.

AI processing

When you use AI features (food recognition, the coach), the relevant content — your message, photo, or description, plus the minimal context needed to answer, such as today's remaining macros and your goal — is sent to Anthropic's API for processing. It is used to generate your answer, not for advertising.

Your health data, specifically

Weight, sleep, nutrition, and supplement logs are sensitive. We treat them that way: they are never shared with third parties except the processors named above (Supabase to store, Anthropic to answer your AI requests), never sold, and never used for anything other than showing you your own progress and powering your own plan.

Your rights

  • Access — everything you've logged is visible in the app itself.
  • Correction — you can edit or delete individual entries in the app.
  • Deletion — email us and we will permanently delete your account and all associated data.
  • Export — email us and we will send you a copy of your data.

Cookies and tracking

The app uses browser storage on your own device to keep you signed in. There are no third-party cookies, trackers, or ad pixels.

Children

Lucky Numbers is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from minors.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will note the new effective date here. Continued use after a change means you accept it.

Contact

Privacy questions, deletion, or export requests: support@luckyfit.org
